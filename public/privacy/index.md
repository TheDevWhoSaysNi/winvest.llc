# Privacy

Canonical page: https://www.winvest.llc/privacy/

Winvest LLC runs winvest.llc as a brochure site. There are no accounts and no analytics or advertising cookies. The contact form opens the visitor’s own email app. Nothing typed into that form is stored on the site.

If someone emails, calls, or sends a message, Winvest receives whatever they choose to send, typically a name, company, email address, and a project description. That information is used to respond and to deliver requested work. It is not sold.

Business plans, financials, and offering details shared with Winvest are treated as confidential. Winvest will sign a mutual NDA before a client shares sensitive material.

The site is served from Cloudflare’s network. Cloudflare may process technical request data, such as IP address, user agent, and timestamps, to deliver and protect the site.
