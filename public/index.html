Raising capital

Let your customers become your investors.

Crowdfunding lets everyday people, not just venture firms, invest in companies they believe in. We help you choose the right path, build an offering investors trust, and run the campaign that gets it funded.

Regulation Crowdfunding

Reg CF

Up to $5M per 12 months

Raise from anyone, customers and fans included, through an SEC-registered funding portal or broker-dealer.

Great forConsumer brands, restaurants, local businesses, and community-backed startups.

Regulation A+

Reg A+

Up to $75M per 12 months

A larger public offering, open to the general public once the SEC qualifies it. More preparation, a much bigger ceiling.

Great forGrowth-stage companies with traction and a story ready for a wide audience.

Regulation D

Reg D

No set cap on raise size

A private placement, typically with accredited investors such as angels, family offices, and private networks.

Great forFounders raising from people they know or can reach directly.

  1. 01

    Readiness

    We review the business, the numbers, and the goal, then recommend the path that fits.

  2. 02

    Build the offering

    Pitch deck, campaign page, video, financial story, and data room, drafted fast with AI and polished by people.

  3. 03

    Launch & promote

    Ads, email, social, PR, and investor outreach to drive traffic while the raise is live.

  4. 04

    Close & grow

    Investor updates, a website, IT, and a sales team that turn new capital into revenue.

Limits reflect current SEC rules and can change. Winvest is not a broker-dealer or funding portal. We coordinate with registered intermediaries and securities counsel wherever an offering requires them.

FAQ

Good questions.

Don’t see yours? Email info@winvest.llc or call 888-727-1119.

Do I have to be raising money to work with you?

Not at all. Plenty of clients come to us just for a website, IT help, ads, or a sales process. Capital raising is one of the things we do, not a requirement.

Are you a broker-dealer or funding portal?

No. Winvest is a consulting and services firm. When an offering requires an SEC-registered funding portal, broker-dealer, or securities attorney, we work alongside them and help you find the right fit.

Which raise is right for me: Reg CF, A+, or D?

It depends on how much you need, who you want to raise from, and how much preparation you can take on. Reg CF suits community-backed raises up to $5M, Reg A+ suits larger public raises, and Reg D suits private rounds with accredited investors. We’ll walk you through it on a free call.

How does AI make you cheaper?

A big part of any project is research, first drafts, repetitive setup, and data crunching. AI tools do that in a fraction of the time. Our people spend their hours on strategy and finishing work, and we pass the savings on to you.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. We treat your financials, plans, and data as confidential, and we’re happy to sign an NDA. If you’d prefer we keep AI tools off your project, just say so.

I’m not in Tampa. Can you still help?

Yes. Almost everything we do is remote, and we work with clients across the U.S. and beyond. If you’re local, we’re glad to meet in person.

How do we get started?

Send us a note or call. We’ll set up a free call, learn what you’re building, and come back with a plan and a clear quote before any work starts.
